5. AI-generated content / AI生成物について
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Some card, relic and UI icons were drafted with Google Gemini (image
generation) and then converted to the game's pixel-art palette, downscaled
and outlined. Characters, enemies, tiles, sound effects and music are the
CC0 assets listed above (not AI-generated). The game's design and code were
produced with an AI assistant (Claude by Anthropic).
